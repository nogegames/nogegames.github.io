Hi! I'm Noge, a solo videogame developer from Spain.

[Hanafuda Koi-koi Dojo](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.Noge.HanafudaKoikoi) is my first game as an independent developer. In the game you can learn how to play koi-koi with hanafuda cards, a classic japanese game. Complete the adventure mode and compete aganist other players in the online mode!
